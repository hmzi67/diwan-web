      Get started

      Three steps from licence key to your first bill. Most shops are up and running in five minutes.

      1. 1

        Copy your licence key

        You’ll paste this into Diwan the first time it opens.

      2. 2

        Download & install

        One licence covers every platform. Pick the device your counter runs on.

      3. 3

        Activate on your device

        Activation needs the internet once. After that Diwan works fully offline.

        1. Open the downloaded .exe and follow the installer.
        2. Launch Diwan POS from the Start menu.
        3. Paste your licence key when asked and click Activate.
        1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Diwan into Applications.
        2. First launch: right-click Diwan and choose Open.
        3. Paste your licence key when asked and click Activate.
        1. AppImage: make it executable (chmod +x Diwan_*.AppImage) and run it. .deb / .rpm: install with sudo apt install ./Diwan_*.deb or sudo dnf install ./Diwan-*.rpm.
        2. Launch Diwan POS from your applications menu.
        3. Paste your licence key when asked and click Activate.
        1. Open the downloaded .apk. If prompted, allow installs from this source.
        2. Open Diwan POS from your app drawer.
        3. Paste your licence key when asked and tap Activate.

      Common questions

      My licence email didn’t arrive

      Your key is always on the Licences page. Use Email me my key there to resend it.

      How do I move to a new device?

      Licences bind to one device. Choose Request device change on your licence and we’ll move it within a business day.

      Can I get a refund?

      Yes — a full refund within 14 days if your key hasn’t been activated. See the Refund Policy.

      Does Diwan work offline?

      Yes. It needs the internet once to activate; after that billing, inventory and printing all work offline.
