FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version. The full detail lives in the docs.

Will Diwan run on an old computer?+

Very likely. There are 32-bit and 64-bit Windows builds for Windows 10 or later, and Linux packages (.AppImage, .deb, .rpm) for 64-bit machines. The same licence key works on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Does stock update automatically when I sell?+

Yes. Every sale decrements stock as it is rung up, on every plan, so the count on the screen matches the shelf.

Can I add more counters later?+

Yes. Each licence covers an agreed number of devices, which our team can raise — or move you to a bigger plan — when you send a request from your dashboard.

Does Diwan integrate with FBR, PRA or SRB?+

No. Diwan does not report sales to FBR, PRA or SRB, and its receipts do not carry a tax-authority invoice number. If your shop must be integrated with a tax authority's POS system, check that before you buy, and email us if you are unsure.
