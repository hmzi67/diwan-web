1. Downloading your installer

Paste your licence key below to get a single-use download link. The link is valid for 15 minutes and works without signing in — the key is what proves the purchase.

The same form lives on the home page, and signed-in customers can re-issue a link from the dashboard. Full delivery details — timings, what the email contains, what to expect — are in the Service Delivery Policy.

2. If your email did not arrive

Payment confirmation normally delivers your key and installer within a minute. If it has not turned up:

  1. Check spam and promotions — receipt mail from a sole proprietorship often lands there.
  2. Confirm the payment actually completed with your gateway or bank.
  3. Email hamzawaheed057@gmail.com with your order reference and we will resend it.

See §4 of the Service Delivery Policy for the full procedure, and §11 for what happens if we cannot deliver at all — in that case you are refunded in full.

3. Activating your licence

Activation happens online, once, on first launch. After that Diwan runs offline and does not phone home. One licence key is bound to one device at a time.

  • Your key is entered once, inside the app, on first run.
  • A bound key activates the same device again with no friction.
  • A different device is rejected until the licence is moved (see below).
  • Repeated failed attempts from one address are throttled to protect the key.

The contractual detail sits in §6 of the Service Delivery Policy.

4. Moving to a new device

Device moves are done manually, by us — there is no self-service transfer button. Email support with your licence key (or your order reference) and the move is normally completed within one business day.

This is deliberate: an automated transfer would let anyone holding a leaked key move the licence onto their own machine. Read §7 Moving to a different device.

5. Getting updates

Updates ship as new installers. Download them with the same licence key you already own — no repurchase while your plan is active.

Details and the update policy are in §9 of the Service Delivery Policy.

6. System requirements

WindowsWindows 10 or later, 64-bit or 32-bit. Delivered as an .exe installer. USB and network printers.
macOSmacOS 11 (Big Sur) or later, separate builds for Intel and Apple Silicon. Delivered as a .dmg.

AndroidAndroid 8.0 or later. Delivered as an .apk. Bluetooth and network printers.
NetworkRequired once, for activation. Not required to sell.
