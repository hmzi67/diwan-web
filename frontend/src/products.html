Products & Plans

Everything Diwan sells, and everything included with it. Diwan POS is downloadable point-of-sale software for retail, restaurants and pharmacies, offered as three monthly plans. Every licence includes activation, updates and support; the plan decides which modules and apps it unlocks.

Plans

Request access

Three monthly plans in Pakistani Rupees. There is no online payment: request the plan you want and our team approves it and activates your licence. Each plan builds on the one before it.

Platform builds

Included with every licence

Diwan runs on four platforms. These are not sold separately — one licence key unlocks all four, so you can run Windows or Linux at the counter and Android on a handheld from the same purchase. Choose your platform when you download.

Item 04

Diwan POS — Windows Build

The full desktop application, keyboard-first, for counter terminals and back-office machines.

  • Windows 10 or later, 64-bit or 32-bit
  • Delivered as an .exe installer
  • USB and network printer support
Download

Item 05

Diwan POS — macOS Build

The same desktop application for Mac hardware, with a build for each of Intel and Apple Silicon.

  • macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later
  • Delivered as a .dmg installer
  • Separate Apple Silicon and Intel builds
Download

Item 06

Diwan POS — Linux Build

The same desktop application for Linux counters and back-office machines, packaged for every major distribution.

  • 64-bit (x86_64) distributions
  • Delivered as .AppImage, .deb or .rpm
  • AppImage runs on any distribution
Download

Item 07

Diwan POS — Android Build

For handheld order-taking and mobile counters, on phones and tablets.

  • Android 8.0 or later
  • Delivered as an .apk installer
  • Bluetooth and network printer support

Included services

Included with every licence

These services come with every licence at no additional charge. They are listed here so you can see exactly what a purchase includes — they are not separately priced add-ons.

Item 08

Licence activation & device binding

Your key is activated once, online, on first launch. After that Diwan runs entirely offline. If you replace a device, we move your licence for you — usually within one business day.

  • One-time online activation
  • One device per licence key
  • Manual device transfers on request

Item 09

Software updates & maintenance

New versions are published as updated installers and downloaded with the same licence key you already have.

  • Downloaded with your existing key
  • No re-purchase required within your entitlement
