FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version. The full detail lives in the docs.

Is there a subscription?+

No. Diwan is a one-time purchase — Rs 8,000, Rs 12,000 or Rs 25,000 depending on the edition — with no automatic renewal. Updates are downloaded later with the key you already own, at no extra cost.

Does it need the internet to sell?+

No. The network is required once, for activation. After that the till bills, tracks stock and prints without a connection, and sync resumes by itself when the line returns.

How do I get it after paying?+

Your licence key and installer link are emailed within about a minute of confirmed payment, and both stay in your dashboard. There is no physical product and nothing to ship.

What if the licence email never arrives?+

Check spam and promotions first — receipt mail from a sole proprietorship often lands there. If it is not there, confirm the payment completed with your bank, then email your order reference and we will resend it.

Can I move my licence to a new computer?+

Yes. A key binds to one device at a time, and moves are done by us rather than a self-service button — that is deliberate, so a leaked key cannot be moved by a stranger. Email support with your key or order reference; moves normally complete within one business day.

What if it does not work for my shop?+

Ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase, as long as the key has not been activated. You may download and inspect the software first — only activation closes the window, and no reason is required.

Do you see my sales data?+

No. Diwan is offline-first: your sales, stock and customers stay on your own machine and are never uploaded to us. We keep only the account, order and licence records needed to sell and support the software — detailed in the Privacy Policy.
