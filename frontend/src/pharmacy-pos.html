FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version. The full detail lives in the docs.

Does Diwan have pharmacy-specific features?+

Diwan is a general point of sale: billing, inventory, reporting and printing. If you need something specific, such as batch or expiry tracking, email us before you buy and we will tell you plainly whether Diwan does it today.

Can I try it before I commit?+

Yes. Create a free account and request the plan you want — we'll talk it through with you before switching on your licence, and nothing is charged online.

Is customer and sales data sent to Diwan?+

Yes, when a counter is online. Your stock, sales and customers sync to your business workspace on our server so every counter shares one inventory and your dashboard can show reports. Billing keeps working offline, and staff PINs are sealed so we cannot read them.

Does Diwan integrate with FBR, PRA or SRB?+

No. Diwan does not report sales to FBR, PRA or SRB, and its receipts do not carry a tax-authority invoice number. If your pharmacy must be integrated with a tax authority's POS system, check that before you buy, and email us if you are unsure.
