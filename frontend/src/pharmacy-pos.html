FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version. The full detail lives in the docs.

Does Diwan have pharmacy-specific features?+

Diwan is a general point of sale: billing, inventory, reporting and printing. If you need something specific, such as batch or expiry tracking, email us before you buy and we will tell you plainly whether Diwan does it today.

Can I try it before I commit?+

Yes. Create a free account and start a 30-day trial on one device, with no payment required. If you buy later, a full refund is available within 14 days of purchase as long as the key has not been activated.

Is customer and sales data sent to Diwan?+

No. Your sales, stock and customers stay on your own machine. We keep only the account, order and licence records needed to sell and support the software.
