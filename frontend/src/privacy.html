Privacy Policy

This is a placeholder. It is not a legal document and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It is here so the sign-up form does not link to a page that does not exist. Replace it before launch.

What we collect

Your email address and phone number, which you give us at sign-up and checkout; your orders and licence keys; and basic technical records such as IP addresses attached to sign-in and download attempts, which we keep in order to rate-limit abuse.

What we do not store

We do not store your password — only a one-way hash of it. We do not store card details: payments are handled by the payment gateway, and card numbers never reach our servers.

Who we share it with

Our payment gateway, in order to take payment, and our email provider, in order to send you licence keys and sign-in links. We do not sell your data.
