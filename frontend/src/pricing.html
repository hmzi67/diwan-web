FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version. The full detail lives in the docs.

How does billing work?+

Each plan is priced per month. There is no online payment: you request a plan, our team approves it and agrees the payment with you directly, and your licence runs for the approved period. Renew from your dashboard; updates come with the same key.

Does it need the internet to sell?+

No. The network is required once, for activation. After that the till bills, tracks stock and prints without a connection, and sync resumes by itself when the line returns.

How do I get it after my plan is approved?+

As soon as we approve your plan request, your licence key and installer link appear in your dashboard, and we email them to you too. There is no physical product and nothing to ship.

What if the licence email never arrives?+

Check spam and promotions first — receipt mail from a sole proprietorship often lands there. Your key is always in your dashboard too. If you still need the email, contact support and we will resend it.

Can I move my licence to a new computer?+

Yes. A key binds to one device at a time, and moves are done by us rather than a self-service button — that is deliberate, so a leaked key cannot be moved by a stranger. Email support with your key or order reference; moves normally complete within one business day.

What if it does not work for my shop?+

Talk to us before you commit — nothing is charged online, and a request can be cancelled from your dashboard while it is still waiting for approval. For licences already issued, see the refund policy.

Do you see my sales data?+

Yes. Your business data is stored on our server: when a counter is online it syncs your products, stock, sales, customers, staff and settings to your business workspace, so every counter shares one inventory and your dashboard can show your reports. Billing still works offline and catches up when the connection returns. Staff PINs are sealed on the counter and we cannot read them. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
