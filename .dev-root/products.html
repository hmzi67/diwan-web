Products & Editions

Everything Diwan sells, and everything included with it. Diwan POS is downloadable point-of-sale software for retail, restaurants and pharmacies, sold as a one-time licence in three editions. Every licence covers all three platforms and includes activation and support at no extra cost.

Licence activation & device binding

Your key is activated once, online, on first launch. After that Diwan runs entirely offline. If you replace a device, we move your licence for you — usually within one business day.

  • One-time online activation
  • One device per licence key
  • Manual device transfers on request
